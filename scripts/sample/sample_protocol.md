Protocol XYZ-201: A Phase 2, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of XYZ-123 in Adults with Moderate-to-Severe Ulcerative Colitis

Synopsis
XYZ-123 is an oral, selective inhibitor of the interleukin-23 signaling pathway. This study evaluates the efficacy and safety of two dose levels of XYZ-123 versus placebo over a 12-week induction period in adults with moderate-to-severe ulcerative colitis who have had an inadequate response to conventional therapy.

1 Objectives
The primary objective is to evaluate the efficacy of XYZ-123 versus placebo in inducing clinical remission at Week 12. Secondary objectives include clinical response, endoscopic improvement, and safety and tolerability.

2 Endpoints
The primary endpoint is the proportion of participants in clinical remission at Week 12, defined by the modified Mayo score (stool frequency subscore of 0 or 1, rectal bleeding subscore of 0, and endoscopic subscore of 0 or 1). Secondary endpoints include clinical response at Week 12 (decrease in modified Mayo score of at least 2 points and at least 30 percent from baseline), endoscopic improvement at Week 12 (endoscopic subscore of 0 or 1), and change from baseline in fecal calprotectin.

3 Study Design
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study. Approximately 240 participants will be randomized 1:1:1 to XYZ-123 100 mg once daily, XYZ-123 300 mg once daily, or placebo. Randomization will be stratified by prior biologic exposure (yes/no) and baseline corticosteroid use. The induction period is 12 weeks followed by a 4-week safety follow-up.

4 Study Population
Inclusion Criteria
Adults aged 18 to 75 years with a diagnosis of ulcerative colitis for at least 3 months, a modified Mayo score of 5 to 9 with an endoscopic subscore of at least 2, and documented inadequate response, loss of response, or intolerance to at least one conventional therapy or one biologic.
Exclusion Criteria
Crohn's disease, indeterminate colitis, toxic megacolon, colectomy, active infection including tuberculosis, and use of an investigational agent within 30 days.

5 Statistical Considerations
Sample Size
Assuming a clinical remission rate of 10 percent for placebo and 28 percent for XYZ-123 300 mg, 80 participants per arm provide approximately 85 percent power at a two-sided alpha of 0.05 after adjusting for multiplicity across the two dose comparisons using a Hochberg procedure.
Analysis Populations
The primary efficacy analysis will use the intent-to-treat population with non-responder imputation for missing data. Safety will be summarized in all randomized participants who received at least one dose.

6 Safety Assessments
Adverse events will be collected at every visit and coded using MedDRA. Laboratory assessments include hematology, chemistry, and lipid panel at baseline, Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12. An independent data monitoring committee will review unblinded safety data after 120 participants complete Week 4.
